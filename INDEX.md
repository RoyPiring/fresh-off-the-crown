## How to read a row

- **Subject** is the tool or the ecosystem the capability belongs to. The list it can come from is closed on purpose: a thing already in daily use, reached through a flat-rate subscription already held. That closure is half the noise filter.
- **Theme** is what kind of knowledge the entry produced, independent of any one vendor: how a run is costed, what goes into the context window and what stays out, how you know an agent got better, the shape of the orchestration, the contract between an agent and the world, and how things fail. A theme widens the subject and never the evidence bar.
- **Friction** is the numbered real-work problem the entry points at, with a measured before. An entry that joins to no friction does not enter the queue.
- **Verdict** is one of adopt, reject, watch, or park. Watch and park both carry a re-check date, and a re-check jumps the queue ahead of any news.

## What is deliberately excluded

Benchmark leaderboards, funding and company news, and anything whose only source is a thread or an aggregator. They match no friction and they change nothing about how the work gets done.

Anything needing a metered API key or a card is refused outright. The practice runs on capability already paid for, which is what keeps a daily habit from carrying a daily cost.
